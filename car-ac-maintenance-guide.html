Car AC Repair and Maintenance in Dhaka

A properly working car air conditioner makes driving more comfortable in Dhaka's hot and humid weather. If your car AC stops cooling effectively, produces an unpleasant smell or makes unusual noises, it may need inspection and maintenance.

1. Why Is Your Car AC Not Cooling Properly?

Weak cooling can have several causes, including a dirty cabin air filter, a faulty cooling fan, restricted airflow, refrigerant leakage or a problem with the compressor. A qualified technician can diagnose the actual cause.

2. Check the Cabin Air Filter

A dirty cabin air filter can reduce airflow through the AC vents. Inspect and replace the filter according to the vehicle manufacturer's maintenance schedule, especially when driving in dusty conditions.

3. Deal with Unpleasant AC Smells

A musty smell may be associated with moisture, dirt or microbial growth in the ventilation system. Have the system inspected and cleaned using appropriate automotive products. Persistent smells require professional diagnosis.

4. Keep the Condenser Area Clean

Dust and debris around the AC condenser can interfere with heat transfer. Ask a technician to inspect it and clean it safely when necessary. Avoid bending the delicate fins or spraying high-pressure water directly into sensitive components.

5. Do Not Ignore Unusual Noises

Squealing, rattling or grinding noises when the AC operates may indicate a problem with a fan, belt, compressor or another component. Arrange an inspection rather than continuing to operate a system that makes severe noises.

6. Refrigerant Needs Professional Handling

If cooling performance drops because of refrigerant loss, the leak should be identified and repaired. Refrigerant should not simply be added repeatedly without checking for leaks. Use a qualified automotive AC technician and the refrigerant specified for your vehicle.

7. Use Your AC Correctly

Park in the shade when possible, keep the cabin filter maintained and ensure the ventilation system is operating normally. Follow your owner's manual for AC operation and maintenance recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my car AC cool slowly?

Possible causes include restricted airflow, high cabin heat, a cooling fan issue or a refrigerant-system fault. An inspection is needed to identify the cause.

How often should I service my car AC?

Follow the manufacturer's recommendations and arrange an inspection if cooling weakens, airflow decreases, unusual smells develop or noises occur.

Can I refill AC gas myself?

Professional diagnosis is recommended. Incorrect refrigerant handling or charging can damage equipment and may create safety or environmental hazards.

Safety tip: Do not open pressurised AC lines or attempt repairs involving refrigerant without suitable training and equipment.
