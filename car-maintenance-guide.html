Car Care Tips and Guides for Bangladesh

Car Maintenance Guide in Bangladesh

Regular car maintenance helps improve reliability, safety and vehicle performance. In Bangladesh, traffic congestion, dust, rain and hot weather can affect your vehicle. Following a suitable maintenance routine can help you identify problems early.

1. Check Engine Oil Regularly

Check the engine oil level according to your vehicle manufacturer's instructions. Use the recommended oil grade and change the oil and filter at the specified service intervals.

2. Maintain Proper Tyre Pressure

Check tyre pressure when the tyres are cold. Follow the recommended pressure listed on the vehicle's tyre-information label or owner's manual. Inspect tyres for cuts, uneven wear and damage.

3. Inspect the Car Battery

Check the battery terminals for corrosion and ensure the connections are secure. If the engine starts slowly or warning lights appear, arrange a battery test with a qualified technician.

4. Check the Brakes

Pay attention to unusual brake noises, vibrations or changes in pedal feel. Have the braking system inspected promptly if you notice any problems.

5. Monitor Coolant Levels

Check the coolant level only when the engine is completely cool. Never open a hot radiator cap because pressurised coolant can cause serious burns. Use the coolant recommended for your vehicle.

6. Replace Air Filters When Needed

Dusty driving conditions may cause the engine air filter to become dirty more quickly. Inspect it according to the maintenance schedule and replace it when necessary.

7. Follow a Service Schedule

Keep records of oil changes, tyre inspections, brake servicing and other maintenance. Follow your vehicle's owner's manual for service intervals and recommended replacement parts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I service my car?

Service intervals vary by vehicle, engine type, age and driving conditions. Follow the manufacturer's recommended schedule.

What are common signs that a car needs maintenance?

Warning lights, unusual noises, fluid leaks, poor starting, abnormal tyre wear and changes in braking performance can indicate a problem.

Can regular maintenance reduce repair costs?

Routine inspections can help identify developing problems early, potentially preventing some expensive repairs.

Safety tip: If your car has a serious warning light, brake problem or overheating issue, stop safely and seek professional assistance.
