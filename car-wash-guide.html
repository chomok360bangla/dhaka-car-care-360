Car Care Tips and Guides for Bangladesh

How to Wash Your Car Safely in Dhaka

Regular car washing helps remove dust, dirt and road grime. In Dhaka, traffic pollution and dust can quickly make a car dirty. A safe washing routine can help protect your vehicle's paint and interior.

1. Choose a Shaded Place

Wash your car in a shaded area when possible. A cool surface helps reduce water spots and allows you to clean the car more comfortably.

2. Use Car Shampoo

Use a car shampoo suitable for automotive paint and a clean microfiber wash mitt. Avoid household detergents that may damage wax or protective coatings.

3. Rinse Before Washing

Rinse loose dirt away before touching the paint. This reduces the chance of dragging grit across the surface and causing scratches.

4. Clean from Top to Bottom

Wash the roof and upper panels first, then the lower panels. Use clean water and rinse your wash mitt regularly.

5. Dry with a Microfiber Towel

Use a clean, soft microfiber drying towel to reduce water spots. Do not rub the paint with a dirty cloth.

6. Clean the Interior Carefully

Remove rubbish, vacuum carpets and use suitable cleaners for dashboard and upholstery materials. Keep liquids away from electronic controls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I wash my car?

It depends on driving conditions, weather and how dirty the vehicle becomes. Wash it when dirt accumulates, and remove bird droppings promptly.

Can I use dishwashing liquid on my car?

It is better to use a product designed for car washing because household detergents may strip protective wax.
