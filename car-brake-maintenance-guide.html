Car Brake Problems and Maintenance in Bangladesh

A car's braking system is essential for road safety. Regular inspection and timely maintenance can help prevent brake failure and keep your vehicle safe on roads in Bangladesh.

Common Car Brake Problems

  • Worn brake pads: Worn pads can reduce braking performance and may produce a squealing sound.
  • Soft or spongy brake pedal: This may indicate air in the hydraulic system, a fluid leak, or another braking-system fault.
  • Brake warning light: A warning light may indicate low brake fluid, a parking-brake issue, or a system fault.
  • Grinding noise: A grinding sound during braking can indicate serious wear or damage.
  • Vehicle pulling to one side: Uneven braking may be caused by a brake fault or other vehicle problems.
  • Steering-wheel vibration: Vibration during braking may be associated with uneven brake-disc surfaces or other mechanical issues.
Safety warning: If the brake pedal suddenly becomes soft, braking performance decreases, a serious brake warning appears, or you hear a loud grinding noise, stop driving as soon as it is safe. Arrange professional inspection or towing. Do not continue driving a vehicle with suspected brake failure.

How to Maintain Your Car Brakes

  • Have brake pads, discs, and other components inspected according to the vehicle manufacturer's service schedule.
  • Use the correct brake-fluid specification recommended in your vehicle's owner's manual.
  • Ask a qualified technician to check brake-fluid condition and look for leaks.
  • Pay attention to unusual noises, vibration, or changes in pedal feel.
  • Replace worn or damaged brake components promptly.
  • Keep a safe following distance and avoid unnecessary hard braking.

Brake Problems in Dhaka Traffic

Frequent stopping in heavy traffic can increase brake wear. Smooth braking, maintaining a safe distance, and following the recommended maintenance schedule can help reduce unnecessary wear. Have the braking system checked if you notice any change in its performance.

When Should You Visit a Mechanic?

Seek professional help if your car makes unusual braking noises, pulls to one side while braking, shows a brake warning light, leaks brake fluid, or requires more effort to stop. A qualified technician can inspect the pads, discs, calipers, brake lines, fluid, and other relevant components.

Conclusion

Brake maintenance is a vital part of vehicle safety. Regular inspections and early attention to warning signs can help you identify problems before they become more serious.
