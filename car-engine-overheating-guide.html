Car Engine Overheating: Causes and Solutions in Bangladesh

Engine overheating is a common car problem that can occur during traffic congestion, hot weather, or long journeys. If your car engine becomes too hot, taking the right steps can help prevent serious engine damage.

Common Causes of Engine Overheating

  • Low coolant level: Insufficient coolant can prevent the engine from maintaining a safe temperature.
  • Coolant leakage: A damaged hose, radiator, or connection may cause coolant loss.
  • Radiator problems: A blocked or damaged radiator can reduce cooling efficiency.
  • Cooling fan failure: A faulty fan may make cooling less effective, especially in traffic.
  • Thermostat problems: A faulty thermostat can interfere with normal coolant circulation.
  • Water pump issues: A failing water pump may prevent proper coolant circulation.

Warning Signs to Watch For

  • The temperature gauge rises into the red zone.
  • A dashboard engine-temperature warning appears.
  • Steam comes from under the bonnet.
  • You notice coolant leaking beneath the car.
  • The engine loses power or runs abnormally.
Safety warning: If your engine overheats, stop in a safe place and switch off the engine. Never remove the radiator cap while the engine is hot. Pressurized coolant can cause severe burns. Do not continue driving with the temperature gauge in the red zone.

What to Do When Your Engine Overheats

  1. Safely pull over away from traffic.
  2. Switch off the engine and allow it to cool.
  3. Keep clear of steam or hot coolant.
  4. Never pour cold water over a hot engine.
  5. After the engine has completely cooled, check for visible coolant leaks if it is safe to do so.
  6. Contact a qualified mechanic or roadside assistance if the cause is unclear or overheating happens again.

How to Prevent Engine Overheating

  • Check the coolant level regularly when the engine is cold.
  • Use the coolant type recommended by your vehicle manufacturer.
  • Inspect radiator hoses for cracks, leaks, or damage.
  • Arrange regular radiator and cooling-system inspections.
  • Pay attention to unusual temperature-gauge readings.
  • Have recurring overheating checked by a professional.

Car Engine Overheating in Dhaka Traffic

Heavy traffic and high ambient temperatures can increase the demand on a vehicle's cooling system. A properly maintained cooling fan, radiator, coolant system, and thermostat help the engine operate at its intended temperature. Traffic alone should not be assumed to be the cause of repeated overheating.

When Should You Visit a Mechanic?

Seek professional inspection if the temperature warning returns, coolant levels keep dropping, steam appears, or you find a leak. Repeated overheating can cause expensive engine damage. A qualified technician can inspect the radiator, cooling fan, thermostat, water pump, and other related components.

Conclusion

Regular maintenance and early attention to temperature warnings can help protect your car engine. If your vehicle overheats, prioritize safety and get professional assistance before continuing your journey.
